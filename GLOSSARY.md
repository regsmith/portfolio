# Portfolio and resume

The public professional profile of Reginald Smith, presenting career evidence, skills and selected personal interests for job opportunities.

## Language

**Machine-readable resume content**:
Accurate professional facts expressed in readable, clearly organized language that both people and automated systems can interpret. It is the same public content a recruiter sees, not hidden text or keyword stuffing.
_Avoid_: AI bait, hidden resume, keyword payload

**Responsibility phase**:
A dated scope of responsibility within a formal role, which may overlap other scopes without becoming a separate job title.
_Avoid_: Promotion, job title, separate role

**Formal title**:
The employer-assigned job title, distinct from a functional headline, responsibility phase or current grade.
_Avoid_: Functional headline, responsibility phase

**Stage benchmark**:
A measured timing comparison for one identified stage of a delivery pipeline, not the duration of the entire pipeline.
_Avoid_: Pipeline speedup, end-to-end benchmark
